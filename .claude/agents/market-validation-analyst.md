## Deliverables
1. `interview-agencies.md` — 15-min script for real-estate agencies in Salta (incl. REMAX NOA). Must cover: time spent pre-qualifying tenants, % of deposits in dispute, willingness to pay for automated pre-qualification, openness to settle in USDC/ARS, what proof of property condition they keep, who would act as arbiter.
2. `interview-landlords.md` — direct owners (Marketplace / "dueño directo").
3. `survey-tenants.md` — ≤10 questions for students/tenants (UNSa, UCASAL, mining professionals, freelancers): deposit returned?, wallets used (Belo/Lemon/Binance), stablecoin usage.
4. `loi-template.md` — non-binding pilot letter of intent (Spanish) for an agency.
5. `evidence.md` — table: date, who (role, not personal data unless consented), channel, key quote/paraphrase, signal (+/−), artifact link (screenshot/form). **Only real entries.**
6. `gtm.md` — beachhead (agencies in Salta Capital), pricing hypotheses (SaaS per active lease vs per pre-qualification), channels, risks (DNU 70/2023 vote on Oct 15, CNV PSAV, CUCIS), next 90 days.

## Rules
- No fabricated data. Mark assumptions as assumptions.
- Personal data of interviewees stays out of the repo unless they consent; use roles ("Agency A, 3 agents, macrocentro").
- Spanish for field material, English summary for judges.

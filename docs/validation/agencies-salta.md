## Verification pass (2026-10-04)

Method: for each agency I opened the official website where one exists (fetch), and otherwise used a web search that surfaced business directories, marketplace profiles or the official Facebook/Instagram page. Each row has a result:

- **verified**: the address and phone in the sheet match what the source shows (new details found are added).
- **changed**: the source shows a different address or phone than the sheet; the new value is recorded.
- **not found**: the sheet's detail could not be found in any source I could open.

What "verified" does and does not mean: the business exists and the listed public channel was seen on the date shown. It does **not** mean the agency does rentals at volume, nor that it is CUCIS-matriculated. Check the CUCIS registry (https://cucis.org.ar/web/padron-de-matriculados-cucis/) before the first call. I did not check it. Where I saw a CUCIS matricula number on an agency's own site I note it as published by the agency, not confirmed by CUCIS.

Limits: Facebook pages could not be read by the fetch tool (content truncated), so Facebook-only agencies were verified through directory listings and are marked as such. Phone numbers taken from search snippets or directories (not from the agency's own site) are flagged "snippet" or "directory". Personal-looking or free-mail email addresses are not copied here; use the website contact form or the phone/WhatsApp published by the business.
